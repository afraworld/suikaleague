How Suika League Got Its Name — And Its Culture

It started with ten friends, a game, and one simple belief: the people who were going to be part of this should have a say in what it became.

Written by Afra · Founder's Story · Suika League Origins

Before Suika League became a large community, we were just a small circle of around ten friends. We played together, spent time in VRChat, watched anime, movies and series, and simply enjoyed having a place where everyone knew each other.

When I decided it was time to turn that little circle into an actual group, I could have simply created it, chosen the name myself and invited everyone afterward.

But that wasn't how I wanted it to begin.

I wanted the group to feel like our home — not something people were merely joining after all the important decisions had already been made. So I told everyone what I was planning and invited them to brainstorm the name with me.

Suika League
suggested by Arvi · born from a conversation · built into everything

My friend Arvi suggested several possibilities, and while we were discussing them together, he came up with it: Suika League.

Suika came naturally from the game that had brought us together. League connected beautifully with the competitive world I came from — racing, rankings, improvement and the idea of people challenging one another to become better. The name felt right immediately.

💡 The Name Behind the Name
Arvi (Arvidius)
One of the original ten. Arvi suggested "Suika League" during the founding conversation — a name that connected the game to the competitive spirit at the heart of what we were building. His contribution lives in every instance, every tournament, every player profile. View Profile →

And in many ways, the way we chose it became a small example of how Suika League would continue to grow.

I founded the community and continued shaping its direction, but I never wanted every useful idea, project or solution to have to come from me.

As the group developed, different people began bringing their own skills into it. When we needed more reliable ways to keep instances open, one member stepped forward and created what eventually became our Suika League bot.

At first, its job was simple: help hold our instances. But it kept evolving. Over time, the bot gained its own branded avatar created specifically for Suika League, visible SL elements inside the world, our community tent, additional screens and other fun assets that made our instances feel increasingly like our own space.

Other members helped keep instances alive, create assets, solve problems and develop ideas when I couldn't personally be present.

🍉

That is something I have always wanted to preserve.

Suika League has direction and leadership, but it also has room for people to leave something of themselves inside it. When someone teaches players, builds something, hosts an event, maintains an instance, creates media, sponsors a project or brings an idea that improves the community — that contribution becomes part of our history.

The community didn't grow because one person tried to do everything. It grew because there was a vision — and people were given room to help shape how that vision became real.

Suika League was founded with direction, but built through participation.
