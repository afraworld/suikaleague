About Arvi

Arvi was one of the original ten friends who were playing Suika together before Suika League even had a name. When the time came to turn that circle into a proper group, it was Arvi who suggested the two words that became everything: Suika League.

That name — combining the game they loved with the competitive spirit the community was built around — captured something that a hundred other names wouldn't have. It felt right immediately and it still does today. Every tournament, every leaderboard entry, every new player who joins carries that name because of Arvi.

Arvi remains a community friend and part of the Suika League story. Their score on this profile is preserved from the early period of the community — an update is pending whenever they're ready to submit.
