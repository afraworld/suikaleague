🍉 Who We Are

Suika League is a VRChat community built around Suika Game.

What begins as a simple fruit-merging game quickly becomes something surprisingly competitive. Players develop positioning, consistency, timing and strategy — and discover they can push their scores much further than they expected.

Suika League brings together new players, regulars, competitive players, teachers, creators and contributors in one place. Whether you're discovering the game for the first time or already chasing the top of the leaderboard, there's a place for you here.

Profiles & Recognition

How Profiles & Roles Work

Suika League has two separate systems: profile tiers (how deeply we showcase a player) and community roles (what that person has contributed). These are independent — a player can have any combination.

Standard Profile

All Suika League members are recognized on the site. How they appear depends on their score:

Member · Any score

Name listed in the community — visible as a Suika League member, no full card.

Player Card · 3,700+ score

Full card in the player directory — avatar, score, role, bio, and favourite fruit.

💎
Premium Profile

An editorial recognition — not a rank, and not something purchased. A Premium Profile is awarded when a member’s story, achievement, or contribution deserves a full showcase. It can include a custom banner, holographic avatar frame, signature quote, detailed bio, achievements, tournament history, featured media, and more.

Premium is not only about competitive score. One strong reason is enough — it does not require meeting every criterion.

A member may receive Premium because they are
The #1 Player — who also hosts events or shares knowledge that helps others A tournament champion A Founding Member A Suika Sensei A creator or builder An event organizer A media contributor A long-term community contributor Someone with an important SL story
Founding Members receive Premium automatically — in recognition of their role in building the early Suika League community.
👑
VIP Member

VIP is a community recognition role — not a profile tier, not a competitive rank. It recognises people whose actions have meaningfully helped Suika League grow, develop, or become stronger. Every VIP automatically receives a Premium Profile, but Premium does not mean VIP.

VIP is not awarded for having a high score, being popular, or being present for a long time. It reflects what someone has done for the league.

Contributions that count
✦Building or maintaining instances
✦Developing the SL bot
✦Creating community assets
✦Teaching and mentoring players
✦Helping newcomers integrate
✦Organising events & tournaments
 
✦Creating media for the community
✦Moderating or managing systems
✦Ideas that became SL features
✦Helping grow SL’s visibility
✦Financial support that directly benefits the community
VIP is awarded, never requested or applied for. The league recognises contribution when it sees it. Financial support may count as one path — but only when it directly benefits the community, not as a shortcut.
How Premium and VIP relate
🏆
#1 Player + Premium
Major competitive achievement — Premium for the story. Not automatically VIP.
👑
VIP + Premium
Community contributor — VIP for impact, Premium included automatically. Gameplay level irrelevant.
⭐
Founding Member + Premium
Recognised for helping build the early league. Premium is automatic. VIP earned separately if applicable.

Roles (Founder, #1 Player, Competitive, Sensei, Event Host, Media Contributor, Sponsor) are always separate from profile level. A player can hold any combination of roles at any profile tier.

Learn & Improve

Suika League isn't only about high scores. One of our main goals is helping players understand the game and discover their own potential.

Experienced members and Suika Sensei share strategies, gameplay knowledge and advice directly with other players. Whether you're trying to make your first watermelon or push a strong score even higher — you'll find people willing to share what they know.

Our learning resources, gameplay discussions and library live in Discord, so players can ask questions and learn from each other in real time.

💬
Join & Learn Free · Open to all levels

Competition should make a community stronger — not smaller.

We believe strong players should have opportunities to challenge each other, while new players should have opportunities to learn from the people ahead of them.

Success inside Suika League isn't measured only by a leaderboard position. Teaching someone, opening an instance, hosting an event, sharing knowledge, creating something useful or helping the community grow can matter just as much.

We want people to have room to improve, contribute and eventually help somebody else do the same.

The goal isn't to protect the top. It's to keep raising the level of everyone around it.

Goals & Mission

Where We're Going

Suika League was built to be more than a game group. Our goal is to grow into the largest and most respected competitive Suika Game community in VRChat — a place where players improve, contributors are recognized, and everyone belongs to something real.

700 members by the end of 2026 is a community target — not just a number. It means more players to learn from, more tournaments, more stories, more people who feel at home here.

This isn't Afra's goal for the community. It's the community's goal for itself. Every member who invites someone, teaches someone, or shows up consistently is part of reaching it.

⚖️ Community Accountability
If we don't reach 700 by December 31, 2026, we face a community consequence — decided together. Not a punishment from above, but something we agreed to as a group. That accountability is what makes the goal real.

The specific consequence will be announced and voted on by the community in Discord.
