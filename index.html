🎮 What is Suika League?

A VRChat community built
around Suika Game

Suika League is the official competitive community for Melon & Chill / Suika Game players in VRChat. From casual weekend sessions to top-tier competition, everyone is welcome here. We host regular tournaments, celebrate our top players, publish player spotlights, and keep the fun alive — because Suika Game is simply better together.
