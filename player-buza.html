About BUZA

BUZA is one of the highest-scoring Suika players in VRChat, sitting at #2 on the global leaderboard with a score of 38,407. A regular presence in Suika League group sessions, BUZA plays with the kind of consistency and skill that makes the competition around them better.

Korean is BUZA's native language and they are currently learning English — communication happens across languages in Suika League, and that's never stopped anyone from playing great Suika together.

BUZA represents exactly the kind of player the Friends of the League category was created for — someone who belongs in the community's story even if they haven't signed up for the formal member roster.
