It is always optional

Supporting is never required

Being part of Suika League has never required payment. Every player, member, and community participant is valued regardless of whether they contribute financially.

We built this league around shared love of a game and each other. That does not change.

Patron Recognition

GOD of Suika

A unique profile and role reserved for those who make a significant financial contribution toward building Suika League.

👑
GOD of Suika
Patron Profile
✦ Unique golden profile page with special visual treatment
✦ GOD of Suika role and badge in the community
✦ Named in the Suika League supporters record
✦ Bio, custom quote, and contribution noted on profile
✦ Completely separate from VIP and Premium

GOD of Suika is not earned through gameplay or editorial selection — it is a mark of patronage, awarded to those whose financial contribution makes a meaningful difference to what Suika League can build.

It does not grant moderation power, leaderboard advantages, or influence over community decisions. It is recognition — a way of saying this person helped build this.

If you contribute significantly and want to be recognised as a GOD of Suika, reach out to Afra on Discord after supporting.

👑 Contact Afra on Discord

Minimum contribution threshold applies — contact for details.

What support does and does not buy

VIP cannot be purchased. Premium cannot be purchased. These are earned — VIP through long-term contribution to the community, Premium through editorial recognition of a player's story or achievement. Neither is available for purchase. Financial support does not affect leaderboard position, moderation access, or standing within the community.

There is one exception: GOD of Suika — a special profile and role available to those who make a significant financial contribution toward building the league. It is entirely separate from VIP and Premium, carries its own unique visual identity, and exists specifically to recognise patrons who directly fund league development. It does not grant moderation power or competitive advantages — it is a mark of patronage.
